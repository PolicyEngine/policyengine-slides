# Speaker notes: Analysing the UK Autumn Budget 2026 with PolicyEngine

## 1. Title

Webinar, Wednesday 28 October 2026, 4:30pm UK time. Presenters: Vahid
Ahmadi, Max Ghenis, María Juaristi.

## 2. What PolicyEngine is

A brief view: the rules, the households and the results, and that all the
code is public.

## 3. How we prepared

- Four mock Budgets (28 September; 5, 12 and 19 October): drill issues #6
  (with the drill 1 retrospective), #8, #10 and #53 in the dashboard repo.
- The runbook is `docs/budget-day-runbook.md` (G0 to G5 checkpoints).
- Mark anything still in review as such on the slide before presenting.

## 4. Past Budgets

- Six events from Spring Budget 2023 to Autumn Budget 2025, rerun on
  policyengine 6.10.2 and Microcosm UK 2024-25 (dashboard PR #107). These
  are today's model and data, not what was known at the time.
- Autumn Budget 2025 in review is a separate dashboard (live).

## 5. The analysis: live dashboard

Click the card (or focus it and press Enter) to open the dashboard in a new
tab, then present from there. The three buttons open its tabs directly:

- **Population impact**: revenue by year, households by income decile, the
  map, and young people.
- **Household impact**: run one household live with Calculate; the result
  takes about a minute, so start it before talking it through.
- **Living standards**: the current-law baseline (OBR forecast named on the
  tab) for incomes and poverty.

Come back to the deck with the browser tab for the close.
