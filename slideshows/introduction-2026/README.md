# Analysing the UK Autumn Budget 2026 with PolicyEngine

11 slides at `/slides/introduction-2026`, for the 28 October 2026 webinar:
what PolicyEngine is, how we prepared for Budget day, past
Budgets on today's model, and the Autumn Budget 2026 dashboard's analysis of
nine candidate measures and the living standards outlook.

- `config.ts` controls the slide order and metadata.
- `content.ts` holds the sources, user list, preparation cards and past events.
- `dashboardData.ts` holds every analysis figure. It is generated by
  `build_dashboard_data.py` from `public/data` of
  [PolicyEngine/uk-autumn-budget-dashboard-2026](https://github.com/PolicyEngine/uk-autumn-budget-dashboard-2026)
  at the commit named at its top, so the charts match the live dashboard.
  Rerun it after the dashboard's data change:

  ```bash
  python3 slideshows/introduction-2026/build_dashboard_data.py <dashboard clone> origin/main
  ```

- `slides/index.tsx` contains the slides; `slides/figures.tsx` draws the
  charts as SVG, in the dashboard's chart types, measure colours and years
  (2027-28 for single-year charts).
- `SPEAKER-NOTES.md` gives the presenter track and what not to claim.
