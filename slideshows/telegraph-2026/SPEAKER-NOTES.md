# Speaker notes: conversation with The Telegraph, 9 October 2026

A 30-minute chat with a Telegraph journalist who may not know PolicyEngine.
Keep the intro short (slides 2–4) and leave most of the time for slides 7–9.

## 1. Title

PolicyEngine. Presenters: Vahid Ahmadi, Max Ghenis, María Juaristi.

## 2. What PolicyEngine is

Free and open source. Anyone can use it in a browser. Two views: one
household, or the whole country. Source: the policyengine.org UK home page
("Free, open-source tax and benefit analysis").

## 3. How it works

Three parts: the rules (open code in policyengine-uk), the households (the
Microcosm UK data: Family Resources Survey, HMRC tax records and other
surveys, matched to official statistics), and the results. Say it in plain
words; she doesn't need the pipeline detail.
